# Maplemark 1.0 TEST
Native Java/Canvas Android 8+ Makaro-style numeric-room puzzle.

Fill each outlined four-cell room with 1, 2, 3 and 4 exactly once. Equal digits cannot share an edge; diagonal equals are allowed. The black arrow points to the strictly greatest number among its immediate side neighbors, not along a ray. The black cell holds no number and belongs to neither room. Gold digits are fixed. Tap editable tiles to cycle 1,2,3,4,blank. Twelve unique 3x3 room layouts and clue boards have twelve distinct completed boards.

Saved maple palette and fixed-clue marks, GG007 icon/menu/site/privacy, unlimited undo/reset. Offline, no ads, analytics, requested permissions or account. Settings and completion marks stay on device. External studio link opens browser.
